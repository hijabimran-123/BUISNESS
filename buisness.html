Pharmaceutical Packaging

Cartons that hold up from press to pharmacy shelf

Medicine boxes carry more responsibility than most packaging — accurate dosage information, tamper evidence, and print that stays legible after months in a warehouse. We build every pharma job around those constraints first, finish second.

  • Accurate, print-ready dielines
  • Batch expiry coding space
  • Consistent colour across runs
  • Tamper-evident carton locks
  • Braille-ready layout options
  • Moisture-resistant lamination

Working Management

From brief to finished carton

A straightforward four-step process, whether it's a single banner or a full pharma packaging run.

01

Share Your Brief

Send specs, artwork, or just an idea — by phone, email, or in person at Rana Arcade.

02

Design Proof

We prepare or refine the artwork and dieline, and send a proof for your sign-off.

03

Print Finish

04

Quality Check Delivery

Every batch is checked before packing and handover or delivery.
